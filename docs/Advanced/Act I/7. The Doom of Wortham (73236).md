## Overview
#### Objective
> Find the chapel in the center of Wortham

The sequence begins with conversations in New Tristram with Leah, followed by going and talking with the Ferryman (120357) that sets passage toward Wortham. After teleporting to Wortham, the path pushes through battles toward the town’s center, cutting down Cultists outside the chapel. When Urzel is slain, and conversations 194933 and 194942 are heard then advancing. A unique town-attack monster spawns and is defeated, leading into conversation 120372 and a transition via teleport (119870) into the Wortham Chapel Cellar. There, the search for the Sword Hilt continues: the altar—a table with Maghda atop—is interacted with, and conversations 165080 and 165101 are heard before moving on. The arc concludes with a return to New Tristram, entry into Cain’s house for multiple conversations, and the unlocking of teleport (waypoint) 10.

#### Observed issues
- Text channel stopped once during the Ferryman conversation (120357); probably unrelated. [^1]
- Very special and unique monsters (bosses) have a very large health (and usually damage level), in this case, dark cultists. [^2]
