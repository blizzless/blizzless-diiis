## Overview
#### Objective
> Save the angel from mad King Leoric's torture chambers and reunite him with his sword, no matter what cost.

The quest begins and proceeds into the Halls of Agony Level 1, then deeper to Level 2 before heading out through the Highlands Passage. From there, the path leads into the Cursed Hold to locate and speak with Queen Asylla. The objective shifts to freeing six prisoners; however, progress is impeded by an anomaly where released prisoners respawn and cannot be interacted with, prompting a workaround to advance after releasing two.

Despite the absence of the Warden, the journey continues into the Halls of Agony Level 3 to search for the Chamber of Suffering. The Butcher is slain, the Stranger is found within the cells of the condemned, the cultists around him are eliminated, and finally the Stranger is addressed—revealing he remembers being Tyrael. Return to New Tristram.

#### Observed issues
- Freeing prisoners in the Cursed Hold is bugged: released prisoners respawn and cannot be interacted with; proposed bypass is to advance after releasing two. [^1]
- The Warden did not spawn during the objective to kill him; progression required advancing anyway. [^2]
