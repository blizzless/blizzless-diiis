## Overview
#### Objective
> Deckard Cain was lost in the cathedral when it was struct by the falling star. Discover his fate.

Escort Leah through the Old Ruins and Adria’s Hut, clear the cellar and defeat Captain Daltyn, then push into the Cathedral to locate and rescue Deckard Cain; defend him, speak with him, wait for him to teleport to safety, and return to New Tristram to meet Leah and transition to Shattered Crown.

#### Observed issues
- Leah follower/teleport sync can break at multiple steps; after killing Captain Daltyn (step 51), Leah may be moved to an unreachable, non‑interactable spot in the cellar. If interactions fail, reload the area or use Bypass. [^1]
- Do not enter Cain’s portal before him (step 17); doing so can prevent his teleport and block progression. Always wait for Cain to port first. [^2]
- See Known Bugs > Act I > Rescue Cain for details and workarounds. If blocked, enable [[Bypass Bugged Quests]].
