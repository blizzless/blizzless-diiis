## Overview
#### Objective
> Put an end to the Skeleton King to free New Tristam from his army of the dead

Return to the Cathedral, assist the Templar (Kormac) and defeat Jondar, descend to the King’s Crypt, place Leoric’s Crown to awaken him, defeat the Skeleton King, meet the Stranger, then report to Cain in New Tristram.

#### Observed issues
  - King’s Crypt doors may remain sealed. Temporary workaround: use !doors near 50.
  - Skeleton King encounter:
    - Portal behind the boss can start open; crown interaction frequently fails;
    - #Resolved ~~Boss stats are over-tuned; appears effectively invulnerable without admin assists.~~ [^6]
    - Progression may require !quest advance twice ~~and/or !powerful~~ [^6].
  - Templar cleanup inconsistency on step 44: follower is not destroyed as commented; confirm intended behavior.
- See Known Bugs > Act I > Reign of Black King for details and workarounds. If blocked, enable Bypass Bugged Quests.
