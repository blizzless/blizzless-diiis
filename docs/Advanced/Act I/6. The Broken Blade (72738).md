## Overview
#### Objective
> Find a way to retrieve the second piece of the Stranger's sword from the ancient ruins known as the Drowned Temple

The Broken Blade sends the hero and Leah to recover the second piece of the Stranger’s sword from the Drowned Temple. Along the way, they encounter and aid the Scoundrel, Lyndon, who joins their efforts. Guided by Alaric, a Nephalem spirit guarding the temple, they learn the entrance can be opened only with two beacons hidden in the Festering Woods. The hero retrieves the Beacon of Honor from the Warrior’s Rest and the Beacon of Light from the Crypt of the Ancients, returns them to Alaric, and crosses the water bridge into the Drowned Temple. Within the ruins, the hero explores the depths, secures the key, confronts Maghda’s meddling, and ultimately claims the glowing shard—the second piece of the Sword of Justice—before returning to New Tristram.
