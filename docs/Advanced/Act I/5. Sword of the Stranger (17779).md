## Overview
#### Objective
> The man who fell from the sky remembers only a shattered sword of power. Find the pieces of the sword to restore his mind.

After a brief cutscene, speak with Deckard Cain and Leah in New Tristram, then head to the Wild Fields and into the Fields of Misery to locate the Khazra Den. Inside, you encounter cultists instead of the glowing sword shard; defeat them, speak with Maghda, then claim the Glowing Sword Shard. Return the shard to Cain in New Tristram (you can press T to town portal) to advance to the next quest, then advances to [[6. The Broken Blade (72738)]].
