## About

A **quest** is a structured sequence of objectives that guides a player through a specific narrative or gameplay experience. It typically includes:

- **Objectives** – The tasks the player must complete.
- **Prerequisites** – Conditions that must be met before starting the quest (e.g., level, previous quests).
- **Branching Paths** – Optional or conditional sub‑quests that alter the main storyline.

Quests serve as the backbone of progression systems, ensuring players have clear goals and meaningful rewards while maintaining narrative coherence.

This document keeps the game’s structure clean, tracks observable bugs, and confirms that every quest log listed is verified in‑game.
