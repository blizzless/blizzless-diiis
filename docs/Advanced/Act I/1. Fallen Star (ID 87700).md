## Overview
#### Objective
> A star has fallen in New Tristram. Protect the vile dead that have risen since it fell.

Listen to Captain Rumford, defend New Tristram’s gates, speak with Leah at the Inn, clear the streets, break the northeast gate barricade, slay the Wretched Mother, then report back to Rumford to transition into Rescue Cain.

#### Observed issues
- Quest state desync on load at step 66: state may appear as −1 or 87700, leaving Leah hidden/not following. Workaround: advance a step and back or reload the area; ensure Leah is visible before proceeding. See [[Known Bugs]] > Act I > Fallen Star.
- Handoff to [[2. Rescue Cain (ID 72095)]] (step 26): Leah’s follow state can be incorrect at transition. If she’s missing, reload or nudge the quest state.
- Note: Completing this quest unlocks the first waypoint teleport.
