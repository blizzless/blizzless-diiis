## Overview

#### Objective
> The crown of the mad King Leoric has been lost to the ages. Find it so that you may destroy the Skeleton King.

Aid Haedrig in his cellar, put down Mira, open the northwest gate out of town, reach the Cemetery of the Forsaken, locate the Chancellor’s altar within the correct crypt, defeat the Chancellor, and recover Leoric’s Crown.

#### Observed Issues
  - Critical: Step 37 (searching the Chancellor’s altar across the three tombs) frequently soft‑locks due to DRLG Emu template/portal failures. Use Bypass Bugged Quests to proceed.
  - Rare: Haedrig can be teleported to an unreachable spot during the cellar sequence (step 41).
  - Downstream: Steps 59/61 are blocked if step 37 fails.
