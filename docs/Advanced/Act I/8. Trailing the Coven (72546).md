## Overview
#### Objective
> The coven has killed Deckard Cain and taken the angel who fell from the sky. Hunt them down and make them pay for their actions.

The sequence begins in New Tristram by speaking with Leah. From there, the path leads back via waypoint to the Wortham Cellar, then north into the Caverns of Araneae to listen to a teleport to the Aranea Cave (78572) and advance. Finding the lair of Queen Araneae: a trapped woman in a spider’s egg is addressed before the queen is slain. The Pool of Venom in the Chamber of Queen Araneae is harvested (Araneae acid), which is then used to free Karyna.
